CSM Daily Digest — Skill Release

  A portable daily digest skill for Customer Success Managers. It scans your CRM, email, call recordings, and team chat each morning, scoped to your active accounts, and delivers a prioritized summary wherever you want it.

What it does:

  On first run, the skill interviews you about your tool stack, auto-detects your CRM's ownership and lifecycle fields, verifies your connections (and walks you through setting up any missing ones), and asks how you want your digest delivered and formatted. After setup, it runs daily — surfacing unanswered emails, unanswered chat messages, product feedback from calls, and promises you've made to customers.


Works across tool stacks:
  CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio
  Email: Gmail, Outlook (via Superhuman), or CRM email logging as a fallback
  Call recordings: Fathom, Gong, Chorus, Fireflies — or wherever you keep call notes (Google Docs, Notion, Coda, Confluence, Slack)
  Chat: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat


Key features:
  Auto-detects CRM ownership fields so it only pulls your book of business
  Smart fallbacks: if a tool isn't available as a connector, the skill asks where else that data lives and connects there instead
  Priority ranking (🔴🟡🟢) and trend context ("3rd consecutive day with unanswered messages from Acme") as opt-in extras
  Auto-escalation (always on): items unanswered for 3+ days get forced to the top of the digest
  Delivery to Slack, Teams, email, or in-chat
  Designed to run as a Claude scheduled task (weekdays at 8am) or on demand
